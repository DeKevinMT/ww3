# EONSCAR v2.66 - Strategic Rebalance

Version 2.66 sharpens the globe presentation and makes conquest, defense and escalation more readable without increasing the steady-state render workload.

## Highlights

- Rebuilt the globe around a crisp Natural Earth land-and-water base with transparent political flags, persistent integration visuals and a single constant-radius surface.
- Refined attack presentation with a subtler projectile and a sharp territory-contained red impact wave, while removing redundant impact and conquest particles.
- Shortened integration, removed the resurfacing duplicate border treatment and kept integrated logistics nameplates readable.
- Improved war audio lifecycle, empire combat pacing and high-suspicion AI reactions.
- Strengthened focused national traits, restored Greenland's 50% Arctic advantage and streamlined the four-stage Arctic research sequence.
- Added paid peacetime reserve reinforcement and preserved deterministic save, AI and multiplayer boundaries.

Authenticated schema-22 v2.65 saves, including Alternative Universe campaigns, load directly and are re-signed as v2.66 saves. Earlier supported migrations remain unchanged.
